Our story

A blended family building something of our own.

Blended Krewe Creations comes from a South Louisiana household where two families became one krewe. We wanted the business to feel the same way: different influences, different ideas, one finished piece.

Our goal is simple — make original products we would actually want in our own home, garage, office or backyard. We design it, print it, improve it, test it and only then put it up for sale.

FAQ

Before you order

Are all designs available now?

Some pieces are still in development. Concept renders stay clearly labeled until we have printed and tested the physical product.

Do you take custom orders?

Yes. Email orders@blendedkrewe.com with the idea, colors, intended use and personalization. We'll confirm whether it can be printed reliably before quoting it.

Can you make something with my business logo?

Yes, when you own the business or have authorization from the rights holder. By submitting branding, you confirm you have permission for us to reproduce it for your custom order.

Can I request a college, pro-team logo or copyrighted character?

Only when the required rights or authorization are provided. Otherwise, we stick to original designs and general color or theme inspiration without copying protected logos or artwork.

Will you ship?

Yes. Shipping is calculated from the finished packed size and weight. Local pickup may also be offered for nearby customers.

What are the products made from?

Material depends on how the product is used. PETG is planned for many functional and covered-outdoor pieces. Outdoor products advertised as weather resistant may use ASA for better heat and UV resistance. Material and care notes will be listed on released products.
