Our story

A blended family building something of our own.

Blended Krewe Creations comes from a Cajun and Creole household in South Louisiana, where two families became one krewe. We wanted the business to feel the same way: different influences, different ideas, one finished piece.

Our goal is simple — make original products we would actually want in our own home, garage, office or backyard. No generic resale. No pretending a render is a finished product. We design it, print it, improve it, test it and only then put it up for sale.

FAQ

Before you order

Are these products available now?

Not yet. We're building and testing the first release collection. Concept renders are clearly labeled until we have real printed product photos.

Do you take custom orders?

Yes. Email orders@blendedkrewe.com with the idea, colors, intended use and any personalization. We'll confirm whether it can be printed reliably before quoting it.

Will you ship?

Yes. Shipping will be calculated from the finished packed size and weight so we don't guess and overcharge. Local pickup may also be offered for nearby customers.

What are the products made from?

Most products will begin with FDM-printed plastics such as PLA or PETG, selected based on how the item is used. Material and care notes will be listed on every released product.

Can I request a team logo or copyrighted character?

We focus on original work. We can make color-and-theme-inspired pieces, but we won't copy protected logos, characters or artwork we don't have permission to use.
